feat(mutation): selective unstamp for MacroIntroduced subtrees (Agent experimental rollback, #2176, refine #1893 #2100 #2105)

Make the documented metadata-snapshot/restore path cover the
Agent experimental self-evolution case: when a new macro/agent
step fails hygiene or validation, the caller can now selectively
clear the MacroIntroduced marker (reset to User) on the subtree
rooted at root-id, optionally zero provenance, and clear the
kMacroExpansion dirty bit — without forcing a full batch rollback
or manual dirty clearing. Pairs with the existing
snapshot_metadata_columns + restore_metadata_columns (issue #1893)
for composite-txn safety: caller can snapshot first, call this,
then restore on a bad step. Independent of the macro hygiene
contract check (validate_macro_hygiene_invariants) — this WRITES
the SoA, that READS without modifying.
